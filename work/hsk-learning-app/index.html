What it does

The app follows a 39-week plan through 339 lessons covering 2,026 words and 339 grammar points, with meanings and grammar explanations in Thai. Lessons unlock step by step. Each word has pinyin, a Thai meaning, an example sentence, audio and stroke-order practice in watch and quiz modes. A spaced-repetition scheduler decides what to review, and a library page collects every word from lessons I have passed.

Details I care about

  • Quiz distractors are chosen to be similar to the right answer, by meaning category for meaning questions and by sound for pinyin and listening questions, so I cannot pass by guessing.
  • The word list was cross-checked against the official HSK database, and when duplicates turned up I removed them instead of inventing replacement words.
  • Database security rules have automated tests that run on a local emulator, never against production.

Honest limits

  • Audio depends on the user's operating system having a Chinese voice installed.
  • It uses Simplified characters, while Taiwan uses Traditional characters, so my next step is to add Traditional forms.

What I took away

Building the tool made me a more disciplined learner, and the same principle as my other work applies. A quiz that is easy to game measures nothing.
